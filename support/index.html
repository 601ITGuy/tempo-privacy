Common questions

My devices aren't syncing. Make sure each device is signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud Drive is turned on. Open Tempo's Settings and tap Sync now. Changes usually appear within a few seconds.

Where is Tempo on my Mac? Tempo lives in the menu bar (a small dot near the clock), not the Dock. Click the dot to open it.

How do I add Tempo to my watch face? Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit, choose a complication slot and pick Tempo.

Can I export my time? Yes. In Reports, choose a range and use Export CSV.

How do I delete my data? Delete entries, jobs or clients in the app. See the privacy policy for removing synced data from iCloud.
